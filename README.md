# Shop & Yum

> **Smart Budget Meal Planner, Automated Grocery List & Pantry Tracker for Android**
>
> Developed by **ahyum Labs**

---

## 📖 Overview

**Shop & Yum** is a comprehensive, modern Android application crafted to streamline weekly meal planning, grocery shopping, home cooking, and pantry management. Designed to help users save money, eat healthier, and minimize food waste, Shop & Yum combines smart recipe management with dynamic inventory tracking and interactive step-by-step cooking features.

Whether you are organizing weekly dinners, scaling ingredients for family gatherings, searching for recipes based on ingredients in your fridge, or cooking hands-free with built-in timers, **Shop & Yum** delivers an intuitive, adaptive experience across Android smartphones, foldables, and tablets.

---

## ✨ Features Overview

### 📅 Weekly Meal Planning & Interactive Dish Swapping
- Plan meals across breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks for any day of the week.
- Easily swap dishes with recommended alternatives or custom recipes.

### ⏱️ Interactive Cook Mode with Built-In Timers
- Dedicated step-by-step cooking interface optimized for kitchen use.
- Multi-timer support to track concurrent cooking tasks hands-free.

### 📊 Macro & Nutritional Breakdown
- Detailed per-meal and daily nutritional calculations for **Calories**, **Protein**, **Carbohydrates**, and **Fat**.
- Align meal choices with dietary targets and wellness goals.

### 🔥 Cooking Streak & Money Saved Tracker
- Gamified streak counter for consistent home cooking.
- Track financial savings achieved by cooking at home versus takeout and dining out.

### ⚖️ Dynamic Serving Scaler
- Instantly adjust recipe serving sizes up or down.
- Automatically scales ingredient quantities across recipes and grocery lists.

### 🛒 Smart Grocery List with Aisle-by-Aisle Grouping
- Automatically generates grocery lists from selected weekly meal plans.
- Intelligently groups items by supermarket aisle (Produce, Dairy, Bakery, Pantry, Frozen, etc.) for efficient shopping.

### 🧊 Pantry & Frozen Leftovers Inventory Tracker
- Track inventory of fresh produce, pantry staples, and frozen leftovers.
- Expiration date tracking to prevent food spoilage and redundant purchases.

### 🔍 Reverse Recipe Search ("What Can I Make?")
- Discover recipes you can cook right now using items already in your pantry and fridge.
- Minimizes food waste by utilizing available ingredients.

### 🤖 AI Recipe Generator & Web Importer
- Generate personalized recipes using AI based on available ingredients, dietary preferences, or cook time.
- Import recipes directly from web URLs into your personal recipe collection.

### 💳 Subscriptions & Monetization
- **Google Play Billing v6** integration offering premium tiers:
  - **Monthly Subscription:** $2.99
  - **Yearly Subscription:** $19.99
  - **Lifetime Access:** $39.99
- Integrated **AdMob Ads** for free-tier monetization.

### 📐 Responsive Tablet & Foldable Adaptive Layouts
- Optimized for single-screen phones, foldables, and large-screen tablets using adaptive multi-pane views.

### ☁️ Local-First Cloud Sync Architecture
- Offline-first architecture ensures instant response and uninterrupted access without network dependency.
- Background cloud sync support keeps data backed up across devices.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Language:** [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/)
- **UI Framework:** [Jetpack Compose](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/compose) (Material Design 3)
- **Navigation:** [Navigation 3](https://developer.android.com/guide/navigation) (`androidx.navigation3`)
- **Adaptive Layouts:** Compose Material Adaptive
- **Asynchronous Execution & State Management:** Coroutines & `StateFlow`
- **Billing & Ads:** Google Play Billing v6 & Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)
- **Data & Architecture:** Local-First Architecture with Repository Pattern
