## Overview & Key Features

Shop & Yum helps individuals and families plan delicious weekly dinners without overspending at the grocery store. Key capabilities include:

* **Local Store Price Matching**: Synchronizes prices with top supermarket chains (Aldi, Trader Joe's, Walmart, Hannaford, Whole Foods, Shop Sunday) based on your ZIP code.
* **Smart Meal Planner**: Instantly builds 5–7 balanced dinners customized to your serving size, active cooking days, and dietary restrictions.
* **1-Tap Dish Swapping**: Easily swap out individual meals or click "Build a different week" to generate a brand-new menu while respecting your budget cap.
* **Interactive Cook Mode**: Full-screen hands-free recipe guide with step-by-step interactive countdown timers, voice cues, and complete macro nutrition breakdown.
* **Smart Grocery List**: Aisle-sorted ingredient aggregation with automatic pantry checkoff so you never buy duplicates.
* **AI Recipe Generator & Web Importer**: Create tailored dishes from leftover ingredients or paste any web recipe URL to parse ingredients straight into your list.
* **Household Sharing**: Sync meal plans and grocery lists live across household members using a simple Share Code (e.g., `YUM-8924`).

## Step-by-Step Tutorial & Guide

### Step 1: Store & ZIP Code Setup
* **Matching Local Prices**: Set your ZIP code (e.g., `90210`) and select your default supermarket chain under the **You** tab or during onboarding.
* **Setting Weekly Budget Caps**: Specify your target weekly food spending cap (e.g., `$75/week`). Shop & Yum recalculates recipe cost estimates to keep your total basket under budget.
* **Pro Tip**: Adjust store and budget preferences at any time in **You -> Settings -> Store & ZIP Code**.

### Step 2: Meal Planning & 1-Tap Dish Swapping
* **Weekly Menu Generation**: Navigate to the **Plan** tab to view your active 7-day schedule.
* **"Build a Different Week"**: Tap the primary "Build a different week" button to re-shuffle your entire week with fresh, budget-conscious recipes.
* **Single Meal Swap**: Don't feel like eating a specific meal? Tap the swap icon on any recipe card to view 3 alternative budget-matched dishes and replace it with 1 tap.
* **Servings & Cooking Days**: Adjust serving count (1–10 people) and toggle cooking days (e.g., Mon–Fri) under **You -> Settings**.

### Step 3: Interactive Cook Mode with Timers
* **Hands-Free Cooking**: Open any recipe card and tap **"Start Cook Mode"** for full-screen step-by-step guidance designed for high-visibility kitchen use.
* **Multi-Step Timers**: Tap built-in timer chips (e.g., `Timer: 12 min`) to launch interactive countdowns with sound alerts so nothing burns.
* **Macro Breakdown**: Inspect per-serving nutritional information including Calories, Protein, Carbs, and Fat breakdown.

### Step 4: Smart Grocery List & Pantry Checkoff
* **Aisle-Sorted Shopping**: Go to the **Grocery** tab to view ingredients consolidated and sorted by grocery aisle (Produce, Meat, Dairy, Bakery, Pantry).
* **Pantry Deductions**: Use **"Check Pantry"** to select staples you already own (e.g., olive oil, salt, garlic). Owned items are deducted from the shopping total.
* **Budget Tracking**: Watch the live budget meter adjust as you check off items while walking through store aisles.

### Step 5: AI Recipe Generator & Web Importer
* **AI Chef Generation**: In the **Recipes** tab, enter ingredients in your fridge or a target meal idea (e.g., "High protein chicken and rice under $10") to create custom recipes on demand.
* **1-Tap Web Importer**: Paste any recipe URL from your favorite cooking blog into the URL importer field to automatically extract ingredients and preparation steps.
* **Dietary Tagging**: Filter generated recipes by Keto, Vegan, Gluten-Free, High-Protein, or Low-Carb rules.

### Step 6: Household Sharing & Sync
* **Share Code Setup**: Locate your unique 8-character Share Code (e.g., `YUM-8924`) under **You -> Household Sharing**.
* **Joining a Household**: Enter your partner's or roommate's Share Code in the "Partner Share Code" field and tap **Join**.
* **Real-Time Local-First Sync**: Any change to meal plans, custom recipes, or grocery list checkoffs instantly syncs across all linked devices.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How do I re-run onboarding or reconfigure my setup?**  
A: Go to the **You** tab and tap **"⚙️ Re-run Onboarding Setup"** at the top of the screen.

**Q: Can I use Shop & Yum offline?**  
A: Yes! Shop & Yum uses a local-first database architecture. All meal plans, recipes, and grocery lists work completely offline and sync automatically when internet connection is restored.

**Q: How does Household Share Code YUM-8924 work?**  
A: Share Code YUM-8924 is a unique household identifier. When partner devices enter this code, both devices read and write to the same shared local-first cloud database.

**Q: How do I submit feedback or feature requests?**  
A: Tap **"💬 Submit Feedback"** in the You tab, or email us directly at `ahyum.labs@gmail.com`.
