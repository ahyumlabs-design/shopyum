Core Features

Weekly Meal Planner

Effortlessly organize breakfast, lunch, and dinner for every day of the week. Drag and drop recipes, balance nutrition, and eliminate the daily "what's for dinner?" stress.

Interactive Cook Mode

Step-by-step guided cooking with hands-free interface and integrated smart multi-step timers. Never overcook pasta or burn garlic again.
