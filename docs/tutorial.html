Store & ZIP Code Setup

Matching local prices & setting budget caps
Shop & Yum calibrates ingredient pricing based on your zip code and favorite store chains so your grocery budget is always accurate before you shop.
  • Select top supermarket chains (Aldi, Trader Joe's, Walmart, Hannaford, etc.)

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Shop & Yum.

How do I re-run onboarding or change my store?

Open the You tab in the app and tap the top card labeled "⚙️ Re-run Onboarding Setup". This allows you to re-configure your ZIP code, default store, serving counts, and dietary restrictions.

Does Shop & Yum work without an internet connection?

Yes! Shop & Yum is built with a local-first offline architecture. Your meal plans, recipes, and grocery lists are saved on your device. When you go back online, changes sync automatically in the background.

How do I sync meal plans with my partner using Share Code YUM-8924?

In the You tab, find the Household Sharing section. One person shares their code (e.g., YUM-8924) and the other person types it into the "Partner Share Code" box and taps Join. Your meal plans and grocery lists will sync live across both phones!

Where can I get support or request new features?

We love hearing from our users! Tap "💬 Submit Feedback" in the app's You tab or send us an email directly at ahyum.labs@gmail.com.
